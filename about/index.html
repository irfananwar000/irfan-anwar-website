Hey! I am a self-taught artist based in Delhi. My work flows across many different mediums—from acrylic and digital paintings to handmade works, illustrations, cartooning, graphic design, photography, and experimental filmmaking. For me, the world isn't just a place to look at; it is a living canvas. Whether I am holding a paintbrush or looking through a camera lens, I am always drawn to raw light, honest colors, and the real, unfiltered moments of everyday life. To me, how you perceive the world with your own eyes matters far more than the expensive tools or equipment in your hands. A photograph or a painting isn't just an image to me—it's a translation of how I experience reality. This space is simply an archive of my visual explorations across these different mediums. Because I believe true art has a value that goes beyond regular market pricing, the works showcased here are meant for exhibition, sharing, and appreciation rather than commercial sale. All content on this platform is protected. Please respect the work by not downloading or reproducing any images without asking first. If you want to reach out for inquiries, collaborations, or permissions, feel free to send me a mail or drop a message on Twitter.

Through my work, I explore human psychology, emotion, and abstract narratives, aiming to capture moments of stillness and introspection. Every piece reflects a continuous dialogue between traditional techniques and modern digital expression.
